Docupal - Applied Interaction Design

Introduction

For this college module, we had to choose a topic that we wanted to explore. We were allowed to select any topic. This topic would then be explored using either a "design game" or "design probes." Our team of two decided to create a design game based on the software product development process.

This game was loosely inspired by the concept of Apples to Apples. The video below demonstrates how the game worked:

Watch the video here

The sticky note solutions generated during the design game were collected and analyzed. Based on these insights, we developed a final design solution.

Design Solution

After brainstorming ideas and sketching a simple graphic user interface, I ended up with the following final design concept and prototype:

Watch the video here

This video was created using Axure RP, Final Cut Pro X, and Adobe Illustrator.

Prototyping Process

A brief explanation of how the interaction was prototyped:

  • A clickable prototype was created in Axure RP.
  • A video was recorded while completing a task in Microsoft Word.
  • A custom graphic user interface was edited into this video and displayed on a smartphone.
  • Finally, I filmed myself using the smartphone to simulate the interaction.

Final Outcome

The resulting prototype is DocuPal, an application designed to assist users in effectively managing documentation during software development.
