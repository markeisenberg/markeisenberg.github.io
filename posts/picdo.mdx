# PicDo App Concept

## UI Only

The PicDo app concept was designed to demonstrate simple, flat, and minimalistic UI designs. Inspired by brainstorming ideas for my Final Year Project, this app idea emerged as a to-do list utility that leverages images for better task management.

The UI was created using Sketch 3 for Mac and Photoshop for the 3D card effect. The images below illustrate various screens and interactions within the app.

---

## App Idea

The concept behind PicDo centers around making to-do lists more visual and interactive. By incorporating images linked to tasks, the app becomes especially useful in scenarios such as:

- Shopping lists:
  - "Which brand of washing-up liquid did they ask me to get?" 
    *(quickly checks the image in the app)*
