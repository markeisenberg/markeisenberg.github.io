# The Investigators - Projection Mapping

## Description

This project was part of my M.Sc in Human-Computer Interaction and took place between April - September 2016. The project was a collaborative effort involving more than 15 people, including 10 students from Computer Science and Media Architecture.

Two workshops were conducted with the artist Krzysztof Wodiczko to prepare for the final event. The project was featured as part of the Kunstfest Weimar event.

---

## Role and Responsibilities

One of my primary responsibilities during this project was to manage and reconfigure the WordPress website used for documenting the process. Additionally, I was involved in content creation.
