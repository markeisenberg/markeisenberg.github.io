# Evaluating Experience in Public Interfaces

## Description

This was a second-semester project that involved the creation and evaluation of an interactive public interface. The system was controlled using motion-based interaction, where a person's movement would trigger responses from the interface.

The project was developed as part of the Urban HCI Lab. The initiative was led by two students and one Research Associate from the lab. Two quantitative studies were conducted, involving a total of 60 participants.
