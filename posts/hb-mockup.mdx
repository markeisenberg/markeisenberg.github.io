# Work Experience - Hungry Bungry Wireframe.

## Description

Below are some mockup samples created during the placement. The first was a mockup for a mobile app interface. The second was a mockup of a restaurant's unique page. Both were made in Axure RP. The mockup restaurant webpage was partially usable and was shared using Axure RP's 'Share' feature. Other people could then try out a prototype of the webpage. Below are also some wireframes and mockups to help guide the direction. Some video editing was also done during the placement to advertise the service and explain how it would work.
