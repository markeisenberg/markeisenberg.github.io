# Pixelfeed - Gaming News App Concept

## Approach/Idea

Pixelfeed is a concept designed to provide gaming enthusiasts with a customized news feed experience. Inspired by existing platforms like Google News, the idea expands to address the needs of the gaming community. 

Key features include:

- Game-Specific Pages: Each game or franchise has its own dedicated page, where users can:
  - Follow external links for more details.
  - Discover new updates related to their favorite games.
- Streamlined News: Users can curate and streamline their consumption of gaming content, including:
  - News articles.
  - Streaming links to watch top creators play the game.
  - Links to purchase the game from online retailers.

This app concept brings together the latest technology and design ideas to enhance how users engage with their favorite digital art forms.

---

## Tools

The design process utilized several tools:

1. Hand-drawn sketches to prototype initial ideas.  
2. Sketch 3 for high-fidelity UI/UX design.  
3. Interactive elements added using Adobe XD.  
4. Adobe Illustrator and Photoshop to create logos and visual assets.
